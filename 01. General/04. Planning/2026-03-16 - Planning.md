## Sprint Outcome: Demo on Thursday

#### Agenda:
* Restate sprint goal — 5 min
* What is the actual outcome for the sprint?
#### Review candidate stories — 15–20 min
For each:
* what does done mean
* blockers / dependencies
* missing design info
* confidence / size
#### Sequence and ownership — 15–20 min
* what happens first
* what can run in parallel
* who owns what
* what needs collaboration across US / IST
#### Risk review — 5–10 min
* what could prevent completion
* what needs a spike instead of a commit
* what must be decided outside the meeting
#### Commit and close — 5 min
* Goals
	* Sprint - This is absolutely what we must finish
	* Stretch - If things turn out easier than we anticipate, we can do this as well without impacting anything else
* selected stories
* owners
* unresolved issues
* Confidence
